# Scope v1.1.0

- Previous/next preset arrows now browse factory patches and saved user patches in one list.
- Navigation continues from the selected user patch, including patches loaded from disk or saved during the session.
- Scroll over the preset controls to browse patches. Missing or unreadable preset files are skipped.

Windows: VST3 installer (`DrelvaScope-Setup.exe`).
macOS: Universal Intel / Apple Silicon VST3 and AU installer (`Scope-v1.1.0-macOS.pkg`), also inside `DrelvaScope.dmg`.
The v1 manual is included. Installers contain plugins only.

macOS bundles use ad-hoc signatures, not Apple notarization.
